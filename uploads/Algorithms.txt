1. 3D RESNET-50 (3D CONVOLUTIONAL NEURAL NETWORK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• WHERE USED:
  - neurosense/models/mri_encoder.py
  - neurosense/models/classifier.py

• HOW IT WORKS:
  - Extends standard 2D residual networks to 3D spatial convolutions (Conv3d, BatchNorm3d, ReLU).
  - Processes full 3D volumetric T1-weighted structural MRI scans (NIfTI format, 96×96×96 or 128×128×128 voxels).
  - Comprises 4 bottleneck residual blocks (layer1 to layer4) with shortcut identity connections that mitigate vanishing gradients.
  - Generates a rich 2048-dimensional dense spatial feature embedding via 3D Adaptive Average Pooling.

• WHY WE USED IT:
  - Huntington's Disease is characterized by complex 3D neurodegeneration (atrophy of the caudate nucleus, putamen/striatum, and lateral ventricular enlargement) that cannot be accurately captured by 2D slices alone.
  - 3D ResNet preserves continuous inter-slice spatial context, volumetric depth, and anatomical symmetries across coronal, sagittal, and axial planes.

2. 2D RESNET-50 WITH TRANSFER LEARNING (MRI SLICE CLASSIFIER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• WHERE USED:
  - neurosense/models/image_classifier.py
  - neurosense/api/main.py (Endpoint: /predict-image)

• HOW IT WORKS:
  - Utilizes an ImageNet-pretrained 2D ResNet-50 backbone with fine-tuned top layers.
  - Replaces final 1000-class fully-connected layer with a custom medical classification head:
    Linear(2048 → 512) → ReLU → Dropout(p=0.3) → Linear(512 → 2/3 classes).
  - Processes standard 2D brain MRI slice images (.png, .jpg, .jpeg) normalized to 224×224 pixels.

• WHY WE USED IT:
  - Enables instant, browser-based, at-home screening for patients and families who only possess exported 2D slice images or hospital scan photographs rather than specialized 3D NIfTI volumes.
  - Transfer learning prevents overfitting on smaller medical image cohorts while providing high visual discriminative power.

3. BIDIRECTIONAL LONG SHORT-TERM MEMORY (BI-LSTM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• WHERE USED:
  - neurosense/models/clinical_encoder.py
  - neurosense/models/classifier.py

• HOW IT WORKS:
  - 2-layer Bidirectional Recurrent Neural Network (LSTM) with hidden dimension 128/256.
  - Operates on longitudinal clinical/digital assessment sequences [Batch, Time_Steps, 5]:
    1. CAG Trinucleotide Repeat Count (genetic marker)
    2. Motor Assessment Score (0–100%)
    3. Memory & Cognitive Score (0–100%)
    4. Daily Functional Independence Capacity (0–100%)
    5. Patient Age (years)
  - Processes sequences forward in time (past → future trajectory) and backward (future context), concatenating both hidden state vectors.

• WHY WE USED IT:
  - Huntington's Disease is progressive and dynamic; symptom decline is non-linear.
  - Bi-LSTM captures velocity, acceleration, and inflection points of functional and motor decline across sequential patient visits, outperforming static feedforward networks.

4. CROSS-MODAL MULTI-HEAD ATTENTION FUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• WHERE USED:
  - neurosense/models/fusion.py
  - neurosense/models/classifier.py

• HOW IT WORKS:
  - Implements scaled dot-product cross-attention: Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax((Q · K^T) / sqrt(d_k)) · V.
  - Bidirectional Cross-Modal Streams:
    a) Visual Query (MRI) attends to Key/Value of Clinical Assessment stream.
    b) Clinical Query attends to Key/Value of MRI Spatial Representation stream.
  - The outputs are fused via gated residual connections and layer normalization into a joint multimodal embedding vector.

• WHY WE USED IT:
  - Multi-modal synergy: Brain atrophy visible on MRI often precedes motor symptoms, while cognitive and functional tests reflect real-world disability.
  - Cross-attention learns inter-modal dependencies—such as correlating striatal volume reduction directly with specific motor and memory deficits—yielding significantly higher staging accuracy than simple concatenation or early fusion.

5. GRADCAM++ (3D & 2D GENERALIZED GRADIENT-WEIGHTED CLASS ACTIVATION MAPPING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• WHERE USED:
  - neurosense/explainability/gradcam.py (GradCAM3D and GradCAM2D)
  - neurosense/explainability/visualise.py
  - neurosense/api/main.py & inference.py

• HOW IT WORKS:
  - Computes higher-order (2nd and 3rd order) partial derivatives of the predicted class score Y^c with respect to convolutional feature maps A_k:
    alpha_{k}^{c} = [d²Y^c / d(A_k)²] / [2 · d²Y^c / d(A_k)² + sum(A_k · d³Y^c / d(A_k)³)]
  - Weights positive gradient contributions (ReLU) to produce a normalized 2D/3D spatial heatmap in range [0, 1].
  - Overlays the 'jet' colormap heatmap with alpha blending onto the anatomical brain MRI.

• WHY WE USED IT:
  - Explainable AI (XAI) & Medical Interpretability: Deep neural networks cannot be "black boxes" in healthcare.
  - Highlights exact anatomical neurodegenerative hotspots (caudate nucleus, putamen, subcortical white matter, and cerebral cortex) that drove the model's classification, allowing clinicians to verify AI findings against established neurological pathology.

6. SHAP (SHAPLEY ADDITIVE EXPLANATIONS — DEEP & KERNEL EXPLAINERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• WHERE USED:
  - neurosense/explainability/shap_analysis.py
  - neurosense/explainability/visualise.py

• HOW IT WORKS:
  - Based on cooperative game theory: assigns an importance value phi_i to each clinical feature across all possible feature subsets (coalitions).
  - Calculates marginal contribution: phi_i = sum [ |S|!(|F|-|S|-1)! / |F|! ] · [ f(S U {i}) - f(S) ].
  - Generates signed feature impact scores and SHAP waterfall/bar attribution charts.

• WHY WE USED IT:
  - Explains the exact contribution (positive or negative) of each patient biomarker (e.g. how much did CAG=44 push the prediction toward Early HD vs how much did Motor Score=85% pull it toward Pre-manifest).
  - Ensures transparency, fairness, and trust for healthcare providers and patients.

7. MULTI-TASK PROGRESSION FORECASTING HEAD (JOINT CLASSIFICATION & REGRESSION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• WHERE USED:
  - neurosense/models/classifier.py
  - neurosense/models/loss.py

• HOW IT WORKS:
  - Shared multimodal representation branches into two specialized output heads:
    1. Staging Classifier Head: Linear(D → 3) with Softmax for categorical probabilities (Pre-manifest, Early HD, Advanced HD).
    2. Dual-Horizon Progression Regression Head: Linear(D → 2) predicting continuous progression risk / delta index at:
       - 12-Month horizon (progression_12mo)
       - 24-Month horizon (progression_24mo)
  - Joint multi-task loss optimization: L_total = L_classification(CrossEntropy) + lambda1 · L_reg_12mo(Huber/MSE) + lambda2 · L_reg_24mo(Huber/MSE).

• WHY WE USED IT:
  - Patients and neurologists need more than a snapshot classification; they require forward-looking disease progression trajectory to schedule interventions, clinical trial enrollment, and therapy planning.

8. ADAPTIVE CONFIDENCE-WEIGHTED CLINICAL SCORING & FUSION ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• WHERE USED:
  - neurosense/api/clinical_scoring.py
  - neurosense/api/main.py

• HOW IT WORKS:
  - Integrates empirical neurobiological models with digital assessment scoring:
    a) Genetic Disease Burden: Langbehn et al. formula -> Burden Score = Age · (CAG - 35.5).
    b) Motor Impairment Model: Non-linear penalty derived from digital reaction time, finger tapping speed, and coordination.
    c) Cognitive/Memory Decline Model: Multi-domain decay function based on word recall, sequence span, and visual change detection.
    d) Functional Independence Scale: Shoulson-Fahn translation to daily living capacity (0–100%).
  - Dynamically computes probability distribution across stages and fuses image model probabilities with clinical certainty weights.

• WHY WE USED IT:
  - Allows robust prediction when users only have digital tests (no MRI), when users have MRI without genetic testing, or when both modalities are combined.
  - Ensures backwards-compatibility with legacy clinical scales (UHDRS Motor, Cognitive, TFC) while enabling digital at-home self-assessment.

9. DIGITAL MOTOR & COGNITIVE ASSESSMENT ALGORITHMS (AT-HOME TESTING SUITE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• WHERE USED:
  - neurosense/frontend/src/App.jsx

• ALGORITHMS IMPLEMENTED:
  1. 5-Round Progressive Sequence Memory (Visuospatial Working Memory):
     - Dynamically generates random spatial permutations avoiding identical adjacent repeats.
     - Scales difficulty across 5 rounds: Length 3 (750ms) -> 4 (650ms) -> 5 (550ms) -> 6 (460ms) -> 7 (380ms).
     - Measures Corsi-block spatial span and working memory capacity.

  2. Word Recall with Mathematical Distraction (Verbal Episodic Memory):
     - Displays pseudo-randomized phonetically distinct semantic word lists with fixed study time (15s).
     - Implements a 15-second active arithmetic distraction task to clear short-term phonological echoic buffer.
     - Evaluates long-term verbal episodic recall with Levenshtein fuzzy string normalization.

  3. Visual Change Detection (Visual Short-Term Memory Binding):
     - Displays an N-element geometric matrix (shapes, colors, spatial positions).
     - Inserts a 1000ms blank mask (visual flicker paradigm) followed by perturbed probe grid.
     - Calculates feature-binding retention accuracy under spatial transformation.

  4. Reaction Time & Bradykinesia Latency (Simple Motor Speed):
     - Random delay timer (1.5s–4.0s) to eliminate anticipation artifacts.
     - High-precision performance.now() millisecond timestamp delta measurement.

  5. Finger Tapping Frequency (Motor Speed & Bradykinesia):
     - 10-second continuous tap counter evaluating tapping frequency (Hz), fatigue slope, and rhythm stability.

  6. Target Tracking & Hand-Eye Coordination (Chorea & Dysmetria):
     - 2D trajectory path generation with continuous Euclidean distance deviation tracking:
       Distance = sqrt((x_target - x_cursor)² + (y_target - y_cursor)²).

• WHY WE USED IT:
  - Replaces inaccessible, expensive clinical UHDRS assessments with scientifically validated, gamified digital biomarkers accessible from any smartphone, tablet, or laptop.

10. OPTIMIZATION & TRAINING ALGORITHMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• WHERE USED:
  - neurosense/training/trainer.py
  - neurosense/training/evaluate.py

• HOW IT WORKS:
  - AdamW Optimizer: Decoupled weight decay regularization to avoid gradient scaling distortions.
  - Cosine Annealing with Warm Restarts Learning Rate Scheduler:
    eta_t = eta_min + 0.5 · (eta_max - eta_min) · (1 + cos(pi · T_cur / T_i)).
  - Mixed Precision Training (torch.cuda.amp.autocast): Accelerates 3D volumetric tensor computations with FP16/BF16 while maintaining FP32 master weights.
  - Data Augmentation for 3D MRI: Random 3D affine rotations (+-15 deg), random scaling (0.9–1.1), Gaussian noise injection, and intensity windowing.

• WHY WE USED IT:
  - Enables stable convergence when training deep 3D volumetric neural networks on GPU/MPS hardware without gradient explosion or memory exhaustion.
